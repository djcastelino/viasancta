## Alternative Free Gregorian Chant Sources:

### 1. Musopen (Public Domain)
Visit: https://musopen.org
- Search: "Gregorian chant" or "plainchant"
- 100% public domain (no attribution needed)
- High-quality recordings

### 2. Internet Archive
Visit: https://archive.org/details/audio
- Search: "Gregorian chant"
- Filter by "Creative Commons" license
- Many medieval chant recordings

### 3. Free Music Archive
Visit: https://freemusicarchive.org
- Search: "Gregorian" or "monastic chant"
- Filter by "CC BY" or "Public Domain"
